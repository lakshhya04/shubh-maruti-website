Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information Shubh Maruti Logistics Private Limited ("we", "us") collects when you use this website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other Indian laws that apply to us.

What we collect

  • What you send us. When you email or call us for a quote or a booking: your name, company, email address, phone number, pickup and drop locations, details of the cargo (what it is, weight, size) and the dates you need it moved.
  • Documents for a job. Once we move your cargo, the documents that job needs, such as invoices, packing lists and consignee details.
  • Technical data. This website has no forms, accounts, advertising cookies or analytics. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google's servers when the page opens, and Google may receive your IP address and browser details.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a plan and quote.
  • To plan, carry out and invoice the transport and logistics services you ask for.
  • To meet legal duties, such as tax, customs, transport and record-keeping rules.
  • To keep our records accurate and improve how we work with you.

We use your information with your consent, or for purposes the law allows, such as doing what you asked us to do or meeting a legal obligation.

Who we share it with

Only as far as needed to move your cargo: drivers and transport partners, airlines and airport cargo terminals, ports and container depots, rail operators, customs brokers and government authorities, insurers, and the service providers that run our email and IT. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

For as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, and for as long as tax, customs and other laws require us to keep business records. After that we delete it or make it anonymous.

Keeping it safe

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect your information. No system is perfectly secure, so please avoid sending anything sensitive that a job does not need.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the personal data we hold about you, to correct or update it, or to delete it. You can also withdraw a consent you have given. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it. To make a request or raise a concern, email shivam.soni@shubhmaruti.com, and we will respond. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

Our services and this website are for businesses and are not aimed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
